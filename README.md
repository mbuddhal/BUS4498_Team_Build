**BUS 4498 Team Build**

> **Problem to be solved**: Busy students often lack the time to continuously monitor stock prices and financial news while balancing classes, assignments, and other commitments. The baseline is that students may spend 15 to 30 minutes a day manually checking several websites, or miss important market updates entirely. Our system lets users choose stocks for a watchlist, automatically tracks price changes and related news, and emails a short summary at market open and close so they can stay informed in just a few minutes.

### System Goal
For busy student investors, stay informed about stocks on their watchlist with minimal daily effort, measured by average time spent checking stock prices and news moving from 15 to 30 minutes per day to under 5 minutes per day, without providing personalized financial advice, making trades, or sending inaccurate or misleading market information.

### Who Is Better Off When This Works?

Students with limited free time who want to follow the stock market but have limited time to monitor prices and financial news will be better off when this system works.
